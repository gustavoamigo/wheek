# Story

The game has no text, so the story is told only through what the child sees and does.
This document is the "silent script" – what we want a child to be able to tell back in their own words.

## The hero

**Darling** *(placeholder name)* – a tri-colour guinea pig: caramel, with a white band and a brown bottom.
Round, curious, always a little bit hungry. When happy, Darling can't help but popcorn.

Darling lives in a wooden hidey house with a heart over the door, on a bed of hay at the edge of the forest.

## The feeling

A sunny morning that never ends. Everything in the world is either food, a friend, or somewhere nice to sit.

## Chapter 1 – Breakfast ✅

Darling wakes up on the hay. Tummy rumbles. There's a carrot *right there*. And a strawberry by the tree.
Further along the path, a nose-twitching smell: lettuce!

Up in the apple tree sits **Squirrel**, holding a shiny red apple and looking down, curious.
Darling squeaks – *wheek wheek wheek!* – Squirrel waves and drops the apple. Friends now.

Tummy full → happy jumps → a star.

## Chapter 2 – What's up there? 🔜

Something blue and delicious is sitting on top of a great fallen log. A mossy branch leans against it like a ramp.
Darling scrabbles up – and from the top, over the treetops, sees rooftops and a little tower. **A town!**

## Chapter 3 – The gate 🔜

The path ends at a wooden gate. Behind it, **the Gardener** is digging, humming.
She is very, very big. Darling wheeks. She turns, laughs, crouches down low – a big friendly face – and opens the gate.
A sprig of parsley as a hello present.

## Chapter 4 – Town friends 🔜

The town square: a market stall piled with vegetables, a child on a bench swinging their legs, a bakery with a sunny window sill.
Every human who notices Darling is delighted. Squeak, and hands come down with treats.
The best treat is high up – but Darling knows how to climb now.

## Ending (of the slice) 💭

Evening light. Darling trundles home along the path, the friends wave. Curl up in the hay. A row of stars above the hidey house.

## Characters

| Who | Look | Role | Reacts to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Darling | Tri-colour guinea pig | The player | – |
| Squirrel ✅ | Red-brown, big curly tail, sits in the apple tree | First friend; drops apples | Wheek → waves, drops apple (or sends a heart if no apple) |
| Butterflies ✅ | Pastel | Ambient life | Wheek → flutter up and away |
| Gardener 🔜 | Sun hat, green boots | Opens the town | Wheek |
| Greengrocer 🔜 | Apron, round | Gives vegetables | Wheek |
| Child 🔜 | Small, on a bench | Shares a snack, pets Darling | Wheek, popcorn |
| Baker 🔜 | Flour on nose | Treat on the window sill | Wheek + climb |
